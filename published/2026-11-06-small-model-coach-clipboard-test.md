> AgentPilot says a 9B model can coach DeepSeek V4 Flash. We read the tables, then ran the coaching idea on our local GLM 5.3 Flash. Half the prompt tokens. A held-out gap of half a task, inside a one-task swing. The card stays on the shelf.
